# yamul — sentence examples

A working catalogue of yamul sentences, organised by the grammatical
device they showcase. Words used here are drawn from
[iconemes.md](iconemes.md) (single iconemes) and
[lexicon.md](lexicon.md) (compounds).

---

## 1. Simple clauses

| yamul | English |
|---|---|
| *xu gar ku* | I see you. |
| *ku gar xu* | You see me. |
| *fon gao* | The water is cold. |
| *xin ciam* | The fire is hot. |
| *xu win fon* | I want water. |
| *xu nerxuim* | I eat. |
| *xu fonner fon* | I drink water. |
| *kaetaor we* | The sun is here. |


---

## 2. Tense, aspect, polarity

| yamul | English |
|---|---|
| *xu pu gar xin* | I saw fire. |
| *xu cu gar xin* | I will see fire. |
| *xu man gar xin* | I am seeing fire. |
| *xu pe nerxuim* | I begin to eat. |
| *xu sen nerxuim* | I have finished eating. |
| *xu rim gar ku* | I see you again. |
| *xu ba gar ku* | I don't see you. |
| *xu ra gar ku* | I do see you. |
| *xu yo gar ku* | I can see you. |
| *xu ko gar ku* | I must see you. (*ko* = NECESSARY here, distinct from *ko* "but" which only appears between coordinated items) |


---

## 3. Questions

| yamul | English |
|---|---|
| *ku gar xu tu* | Did you see me? |
| *ku gar fertu* | What did you see? (THING + ASK) |
| *semtu gar xin* | Who saw the fire? (PERSON + ASK) |
| *ku sim antu* | Where do you live? (PLACE + ASK) |
| *ku cu rehil curtu* | When will you go? (TIME + ASK) |
| *ku reman fetu* | Why are you working? (BECAUSE + ASK) |
| *ku kambil lintu* | How do you write? (WAY + ASK) |


---

## 4. Conditionals

| yamul | English |
|---|---|
| *im fonyim, xu ba rehil xam* | If it rains, I won't go outside. |
| *im ku rehil, xu cu rehil* | If you go, I will go. |
| *baim ku rehil, xu yal we* | Unless you go, I stay here. |
| *im ku ba mi, biltu* | If you don't know, ask. |


---

## 5. Causal

| yamul | English |
|---|---|
| *xu tulman fe xu pu reman* | I am tired because I worked. |
| *xu deltiar fe xu gar xin* | I'm afraid because I see fire. |
| *fe miulcur, xu yalman* | Because it's night, I sleep. |


---

## 6. Concessive (*naim*)

| yamul | English |
|---|---|
| *naim fonyim, xu cu rehil xam* | Even if it rains, I will go outside. |
| *naim xu tulman, xu reman* | Although I'm tired, I work. |
| *naim ku ba mi, xu mi* | Even if you don't know, I do. |
| *naim xin ciam, xu we* | Although the fire is hot, I'm here. |


---

## 7. Purpose (*hil* as subordinator)

| yamul | English |
|---|---|
| *xu reman, hil ku nerxuim* | I work so that you can eat. |
| *xu pu rer fon, hil xin sen* | I gave water so that the fire would stop. |
| *xu kambil, hil ku garbil* | I write so that you read. |
| *xu mi yamul, hil xu bil yamul* | I learn yamul in order to speak yamul. |


---

## 8. Temporal (clause-initial *pu* / *cu* / *man* / *cur*)

| yamul | English |
|---|---|
| *cur xu gar xin, xu deltiar* | When I see fire, I'm afraid. |
| *cur kaetaor rarre, xu pegar* | When the sun rises, I wake. |
| *man ku yalman, xu reman* | While you sleep, I work. |
| *man xin man, ba fonner fon* | While the fire continues, don't drink water. |
| *pu xu yalman, xu yam* | Before I sleep, I think. |
| *cu ku nerxuim, fonner fon* | After you eat, drink water. |


---

## 9. Relative clauses (*il*)

| yamul | English |
|---|---|
| *fonkeu il xu mi* | the river that I know |
| *semli il ku her* | the friend whom you trust |
| *yamul il xu* | my language (*lit.* the language of me) |
| *xin il xu pu gar* | the fire that I saw |
| *bumyar il we* | the house that's here |


---

## 10. Complement clauses (*il*)

| yamul | English |
|---|---|
| *xu mi il ku pu gar xin* | I know that you saw the fire. |
| *xu yam il fonyim cu* | I think that it will rain. |
| *xu win il ku nerxuim* | I want you to eat. (*lit.* I want that you eat) |
| *ku pu bil il xu tulman* | You said that I'm tired. |


---

## 11. Comparatives

| yamul | English |
|---|---|
| *xin sil ciam fon* | Fire is hotter than water. (*lit.* fire MORE hot water) |
| *bumceim sil ceim ceimsim* | A mountain is higher than a tree. |
| *xu sil tulman ku* | I am more tired than you. |


---

## 12. Nested / complex

| yamul | English |
|---|---|
| *xu yam il semli il ku her mi lin* | I think that the friend whom you trust knows the way. |
| *im ku pu gar fonkeu il xu mi, biltu* | If you saw the river that I know, ask. |
| *cu xu pu nerxuim, xu yam il xu cu yalman* | After I ate, I thought that I would sleep. |
| *xu deltiar fe xu pu gar xin il sul* | I'm afraid because I saw a fire that was big. |
| *naim ku ba mi yamul, xu cu kambil il ku, hil ku mi* | Even if you don't know yamul, I will write to you so that you learn. |


---

## 13. A short connected passage

Demonstrating that complex sentences hold together across a paragraph:

> *miulcur. xu yalman bumyar il xu. man xu yalman, xu yalyam il fonkeu
> il sul. cur kaetaor rarre, xu pegar, ul xu yam il yalyam ra.*

| | gloss |
|---|---|
| *miulcur.* | Night. |
| *xu yalman bumyar il xu.* | I sleep [in] my house. |
| *man xu yalman, xu yalyam il fonkeu il sul.* | While I sleep, I dream of a river that's big. |
| *cur kaetaor rarre, xu pegar, ul xu yam il yalyam ra.* | When the sun rises, I wake, and I think that the dream was good. |

The "in" relation in *yalman bumyar* ("sleep house") is unmarked —
yamul drops bare locative prepositions in the same way it drops bare
"at" with time expressions.
